Resources

Here are helpful organizations, communities, tools, and information to support your ADHD journey. Whether you're newly diagnosed, exploring treatment options, or looking for community, these resources can help.

Trusted national organizations with research-backed information and support:

CHADD (Children and Adults with ADHD) ADDitude Magazine National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) CDC - ADHD Information Understood.org - ADHD & Learning

Books About ADHD

Recommended books for understanding and managing ADHD:

- "Driven to Distraction" by Ned Hallowell & John Ratey

- "The ADHD Workbook" by Ari Tuckman

- "A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD" by Sari Solden

For Teens & Young Adults:

- "The Thriving Adolescent" by Ned Hallowell

- "Answers to Distraction" by Ned Hallowell & John Ratey

For Parents:

- "The ADHD Parenting Handbook" by Elaine Taylor-Klaus

- "ADHD 2.0" by Ned Hallowell & John Ratey

Crisis & Emergency Support

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (Call or Text 988) Crisis Text Line (Text HOME to 741741) NAMI - Mental Health Support

You are not alone. Help is available 24/7.

A Note on Resources

This list is just a starting point. Everyone's ADHD journey is different. Explore these resources, see what resonates with you, and build your own support system. Whether it's online communities, apps, books, or professional help—there are people and tools ready to support you.

You don't have to figure this out alone.
